You know what's strange? I spent most of yesterday trying to save time, and somehow managed to make absolutely everything take longer.

It started with the train. I checked the timetable, worked out the quickest route, and left the house feeling unusually pleased with myself. Then, halfway to the station, I realized I'd forgotten my headphones. So I went back. Obviously, I missed the train.

The next one wasn't coming for half an hour, so I wandered into a little bookshop across the road. I'd walked past it hundreds of times, but never actually gone inside. There was a handwritten sign in the window that said, "Come in. You're probably early for something." Which felt a bit personal, honestly.

The woman behind the counter asked what I liked reading. I gave her this incredibly vague answer about wanting something interesting, but not too heavy. She nodded like that was a perfectly useful description, disappeared behind a shelf, and came back with a book I'd never heard of.

I sat outside the station, opened the first page, and completely forgot about the train. And here's the annoying part. That was easily the best hour of my week. No headphones. No carefully optimized route. Just a slightly uncomfortable bench, a coffee that went cold, and a book somebody thought I might enjoy.
